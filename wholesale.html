What ships with every order

Wholesale is a paperwork business as much as a product business. These are standard, not extras:

Product and compliance pack

Spec sheet with dimensions, weight, material and battery data; test reports and declarations for the model on request; material safety data for lubricants and cleaners.

Photo and content pack

White-background and lifestyle images, plus English product copy you can adapt for your own listings — no watermarks, no competitor branding.

Packing that survives customs

Neutral outer carton, no brand marks outside, neutral item description on the invoice, HS codes agreed before dispatch, and carton labels matching your forwarder's format.

QC before dispatch

Function test on every unit in small orders, AQL sampling on bulk, photos of the packed cartons and the pallet before the truck leaves.

Questions buyers ask first

The short answers. Longer ones are in Resources.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ starts at 10 pieces per SKU for stock items. Custom colours and private-label packaging usually start at 100 to 300 pieces per design, depending on the process.

Do you send samples before a bulk order?

Yes. Sample orders are accepted on almost every SKU. Samples are charged at the single-unit price plus freight, and the sample cost is credited against the first bulk order above the MOQ.

How long is production and delivery?

Stock items leave our warehouse in 3 to 7 working days. Private-label production takes 15 to 30 days after artwork approval. Express air takes 5 to 9 days door to door; sea freight takes 25 to 40 days depending on the port.

What payment terms do you accept?

Orders are handled offline: a proforma invoice is issued, then payment by bank transfer, typically 30 percent deposit and the balance before dispatch. Payment links and online checkout are intentionally not used.

Can you supply CE, RoHS or FDA documentation?

Test reports and declarations are available on request for most SKUs, together with material safety data and silicone migration test reports. Ask for the document pack for the exact model you are quoting.

Do you offer private label and custom packaging?

Yes. Logo printing or embossing, custom colour, retail box, insert card, pouch and outer carton marking are available. Artwork is confirmed with a digital proof and, for larger runs, a physical pre-production sample.

Can you consolidate different suppliers into one shipment?

Yes. We buy from vetted manufacturers and consolidate at our warehouse, so a mixed order ships as one carton set with one set of export documents.

How is the goods declared and packed for customs?

Every shipment uses neutral outer cartons without brand marks, neutral product descriptions on the commercial invoice, and HS codes agreed with you before dispatch. We do not misdeclare goods or under-invoice values.
