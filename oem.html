Private label vs OEM: pick the right door

Most first projects should be private label. OEM only makes sense when the existing models cannot carry your positioning.

Private label

Existing model, your brand

Take a catalog model and change what the customer sees: logo, colour, retail box, insert card, pouch, outer carton. Tooling already exists, so MOQ is 100–300 pcs per design and the timeline is 15–25 days after artwork approval.

Best for: first brand, test launch, marketplace own-brand listings.

OEM

New mould, new product

A shape, size or function that does not exist in the catalog. Requires industrial design, a mould (typically 15,000–40,000 USD), and a run of 500–1,000 pcs. Timeline is 45–90 days from design freeze.

Best for: established brands with a clear product gap and volume.

What can be customised

Everything a buyer sees, plus the documents behind it.

Product surface

Logo embossing or printing, custom body colour, matte or gloss finish, silicone hardness adjustment within the safe range.

Packaging

Rigid retail box, sleeve, EVA or velvet pouch, insert card, QR card, tamper seal, barcode and FNSKU labels.

Documents

Your brand on the spec sheet, declaration of conformity, user manual and care card — translated to your market language.

Cartons

Printed or neutral outer carton, your carton mark, pallet configuration and forwarder label format.

Content

Product photography in your brand's style, English copy, and adaptation for marketplace listing requirements.

Compliance

CE / RoHS / REACH test coordination, silicone migration reports, MSDS for lubricants — quoted with the project.

Timeline, step by step

Production

QC + dispatch

OEM / private-label FAQ

What is the MOQ for private label?

100–300 pieces per design for most models, depending on the packaging process. Printing on the product body itself usually needs the higher end of that range.

Do you own the mould if I pay for it?

Mould ownership is agreed in writing before the project starts. Standard terms: the mould is dedicated to your brand and is not used for other clients while your programme is active.

Can I get a sample with my logo before production?

Yes. A pre-production sample is standard on private-label projects. It is charged at sample cost and credited against the bulk invoice.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, before drawings, artwork or target prices are shared. Send yours or use our template.

Who handles certification?

We coordinate the tests with accredited labs and put your brand on the declaration. The certificate holder and the legal importer of record are agreed before the run.
